# Fix Member Transitions Plan

## Objective
To resolve lag during the member card expansion animation, fix the broken unmount/closing animation, and perfectly implement the M3 Expressive morphing of the organic shape into the modal bounds.

## Key Files & Context
- `src/utils/physics.ts`: Defines spring physics and SVG path generation.
- `src/App.tsx`: Orchestrates the AnimatePresence and modal mounting/unmounting.
- `src/components/MemberBlob.tsx`: The collapsed state of the member card.
- `src/components/ExpandedModal.tsx`: The expanded state of the member card.

## Implementation Steps

### 1. Performance Optimization (Fix Lag)
- **Target:** `src/utils/physics.ts`
- **Action:** Reduce the `points` constant in the `getPath` function from `840` to `120`. This drastically reduces the number of Catmull-Rom splines calculated during high-frequency spring transitions, eliminating Main Thread blockage and SVG rendering lag without sacrificing visual smoothness.

### 2. Restore Closing Animation
- **Target:** `src/App.tsx`
- **Action:** Remove the `exit={{ opacity: 0 }}` prop and its accompanying `transition` from the `motion.div` wrapper around `<ExpandedModal />`. This fading wrapper was preventing Framer Motion's `LayoutGroup` from animating the shared layout elements back to their original positions upon unmount.

### 3. Decouple and Perfect Morphing (Fix Organic Shape)
- **Target:** `src/components/MemberBlob.tsx` and `src/components/ExpandedModal.tsx`
- **Action:** Separate the structural container from the visual background.
  - **Containers:** The `motion.div` representing the card/modal bounds will use `backgroundColor: "transparent"`. `ExpandedModal`'s container will maintain `borderRadius: 28` and `overflow: hidden` to clip scrollable content, but the visual morphing will occur via SVG.
  - **Background Morphing:** Implement a shared `<motion.svg layoutId="bg-svg-...">` containing a `<motion.path layoutId="bg-path-...">`. This path will morph seamlessly from the organic (`cookie`/`blob`) path generated by `getPath` into a `rect` path (superellipse) generated by `getPath`.
  - **Visual Effects:** Apply `backdrop-filter` and `filter: drop-shadow(...)` directly to the `ExpandedModal` SVG path to ensure shadows follow the morphing contour perfectly.
  - **Portrait Morphing:** Maintain the shared `layoutId` on the clipping path (`portrait-clip-...`) and the image wrapper (`portrait-container-...`) to ensure the portrait itself morphs accurately from the organic shape to the rounded rectangle.

## Verification & Testing
1. Click a member card: Ensure the opening animation is fluid (60fps) and the organic shape physically morphs into the modal bounds without flashing a circular or rectangular border-radius mid-transition.
2. Click outside or press Escape: Ensure the modal smoothly shrinks and flies back to the specific member card in the grid, seamlessly morphing back into the organic shape.
3. Scroll and Layering: Verify that during the closing animation, the member card remains in the foreground (z-index 150+) and does not slip behind adjacent cards or headers.
